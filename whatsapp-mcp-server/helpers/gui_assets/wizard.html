Before you link

Risk of account ban. This links to WhatsApp through whatsmeow, an unofficial WhatsApp Web client that is not approved by WhatsApp/Meta. WhatsApp may flag, restrict, or permanently ban accounts that use unofficial clients — the risk is highest with automated or bulk sending. Use an account you can afford to lose, keep usage personal and low-volume, and never spam.

Enter your API key

This app and your bridge share one secret. Paste the WHATSAPP_API_KEY your bridge is already running with (it's in the bridge's .env) — the two must match. It's saved only on this machine and used to authenticate to your local bridge.
